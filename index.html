About Us

Welcome to Angelica Handmade, where creativity meets craftsmanship.

Founded in March 2023, Angelica Handmade was created with a passion for designing unique handmade bags that combine beauty, quality, and attention to detail.

We believe that every bag should be more than just an accessory - it should reflect your personality and add something special to your look. That's why we are always exploring new materials, techniques, and designs to keep growing and bringing fresh ideas to our customers.

From cordon and beautifully crafted beaded bags to canvas bags, fluffy bags, and elegant organza ribbon designs, we love creating different styles rather than limiting ourselves to one type of material or technique.

Every piece is carefully handmade with great attention to finishing and detail. We are constantly learning, improving, and developing our designs to offer handmade bags that are not only beautiful, but also made with love and care.
